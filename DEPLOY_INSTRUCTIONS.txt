================================================================================
  KRONICLEZ DIGITAL TV MENU BOARD — HOSTINGER DEPLOYMENT INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

This package contains the complete, ready-to-run Kroniclez TV Menu Board system
configured for Hostinger (or any Apache/cPanel PHP hosting provider).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO UPLOAD & DEPLOY ON HOSTINGER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Log in to your Hostinger Control Panel (hPanel).
Step 2: Go to "Websites" -> Select your domain -> Open "File Manager".
Step 3: Navigate to the `public_html` folder (or a subfolder such as `public_html/menu`).
Step 4: Click the "Upload" button (top right), choose this zip file (`kroniclez_tv_menu_deploy.zip`).
Step 5: Right-click the uploaded zip file and select "Extract".
Step 6: Ensure files like `index.php`, `.htaccess`, `tv_menu.php`, `config.py`, and the `static/`
        folder are located directly in your target folder.

That's it! No database setup or server compilation is required.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LIVE TV SCREEN URLs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once extracted, your screens are immediately live at:

* Dedicated TV 1 (Pre-Rolls & Infused):
  https://yourdomain.com/tv1   (or https://yourdomain.com/?screen=1)

* Dedicated TV 2 (Flower & Vapes):
  https://yourdomain.com/tv2   (or https://yourdomain.com/?screen=2)

* Dedicated TV 3 (Edibles, Drinks & Concentrates):
  https://yourdomain.com/tv3   (or https://yourdomain.com/?screen=3)

* Main TV Menu (with screen switcher):
  https://yourdomain.com/

* Staff & Budtender Admin Portal (Staff PIN: 4200):
  https://yourdomain.com/admin

* Direct Standalone PHP Fallback:
  https://yourdomain.com/tv_menu.php?screen=1
  https://yourdomain.com/tv_menu.php?screen=2
  https://yourdomain.com/tv_menu.php?screen=3

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TV SCREEN KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Press 'F' on the TV / browser keyboard to toggle Fullscreen Mode.
* Press '1', '2', or '3' to instantly switch between screens.
* Press 'R' to force reload live inventory.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. BUILT-IN FAILSAFE / ZERO-DOWNTIME PROTECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The system connects to Tendy POS location catalog API.
* In the event that Tendy POS is unreachable, Wi-Fi drops, or the Tendy password
  changes, the menu automatically serves from `last_known_good_inventory.json`.
* Your TV screens will NEVER show a blank screen or 500 error.
